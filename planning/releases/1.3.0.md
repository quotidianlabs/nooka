# nooka 1.3.0

Tasks that come back on their own. This release adds **recurring tasks** — mark
a chore to repeat, and it returns to your list on its own schedule after each
time you finish it.

## Highlights

- **Recurring tasks.** Edit a task and turn on **Repeat**, then choose an
  interval — every N **days**, **weeks**, or **months**. Active recurring tasks
  show a small "🔁 Every 3 weeks" line so you can tell them apart at a glance.
- **Completion-relative, no pile-up.** The next occurrence is measured from when
  you actually complete the task, not a fixed calendar date — finish it late and
  the next one still lands a full interval later. Water the plants every 3 days
  means 3 days after you last did it.
- **Waiting tasks live in the Archive.** When you complete a recurring task it
  slips out of the active list and waits in **Archive**, labelled
  "Returns in N days". From there you can **Return now** to bring it back early,
  or **Delete** to stop it repeating.

## Notes

- Recurrence is set from a task's **edit** dialog (open the task's menu →
  Edit → Repeat). Add a task as usual, then turn on Repeat when you want it to
  recur.
- "Clear archive" and the 30-day auto-removal only affect completed one-off
  tasks — waiting recurring tasks are never swept away.
- Backups are updated to carry recurrence, and **older backups still import**
  cleanly (they simply have no recurring tasks).
- Available in **English and Russian**.
